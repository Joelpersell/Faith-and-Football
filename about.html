About Faith and Football

Welcome to Faith and Football.

Faith and Football posts fortnightly articles on topics concerning the Christian faith and the beautiful game, most commonly known as football. Articles alternate between faith- and football-related topics.

Whether exploring the theological depths of virtues like humility, or breaking down the legacy of the greatest athletes to ever grace the pitch, this blog aims to connect two great passions. Suggestions in the comments are always welcome!
