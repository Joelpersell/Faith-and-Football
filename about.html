About Faith and Football

Welcome to Faith and Football.

My name is Joel Persell. I am a Christian, husband of the most amazing wife, and father of two beautiful daughters. My career is in corporate finance, but in my free time, I love to write. This blog is my hobby, and I do not expect to turn it into a full-time job. I do it for two simple reasons: it brings me joy, and I hope to stir up the faith and passion of anyone who takes the time to read what I write.

Two of my biggest passions have always been my faith in God and love for the beautiful game. This has been true for almost as long as I can remember. Since I also enjoy writing, you can imagine how much fun it is for me to combine all three of these interests. Now, whether my writing is as good as my lovely wife tells me, I am not so sure; nonetheless, I hope you enjoy following along as I work to unite my love of God and football in the pages of this blog.

Please feel free to leave any suggestions in the comments under each article, letting me know what you thought of it or whether you would like to see me cover any particular topics in the future. I am learning as I go, so any help or encouragement is always much appreciated.

In Christ,

Joel Persell
