About Faith and Football

Welcome to Faith and Football.

Two of my biggest passions have always been my faith in God and love for the beautiful game. This has been true for almost as long as my memory extends. Now, whether my writing is as good as my lovely wife tells me, I am not sure, but I hope you enjoy reading my labor of love, as I work to unite my love of God and football together in the pages of this blog.

My name is Joel Persell, and I am a Christian husband and father of two beautiful daughters. My career is in Finance, and, as you can probably guess, I love to write. This blog is my hobby, and I do not expect to turn it into a full-time job. I do it for two simple reasons: it brings me joy, and I hope to stir up the faith and passion of anyone who takes the time to read what I write.

Please feel free to leave and suggestions in the comments of each article, letting me know what you thought of it, or whether you would like to see me cover any particular topics in the future. I am learning as I go, so any help or encouragement is always much appreciated.

In Christ,

Joel Persell
